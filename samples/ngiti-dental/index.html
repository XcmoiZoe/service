FAQ

Questions patients ask

Can’t find your answer? Message us and we’ll reply within the hour during clinic hours.

Do you accept HMO?

Yes. We accept most major HMOs for consultation, cleaning and simple fillings. Send us your HMO name and card number and we’ll verify your coverage before your visit.

Can I pay for braces in installments?

Yes. Metal braces start with a ₱5,000 down payment, and the balance is paid monthly during your adjustments. GCash, cards and cash are accepted.

Does cleaning hurt?

Most patients feel only mild pressure. If your gums are sensitive, tell us and we’ll use topical numbing gel.

How early should I arrive?

Please come 10 minutes early on your first visit to fill out your dental history form.

Do you treat kids?

Yes. Dr. Joy Lim sees children from age 2, on Wednesdays and Saturdays.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking for 4 cars is available in front of the building.
