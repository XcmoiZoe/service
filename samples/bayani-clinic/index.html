Before your test

How to prepare

Fasting tests

No food for 8–12 hours. Plain water is OK. Come before 10 AM.

Urine & stool samples

Use a sterile container from any pharmacy. First morning urine is best.

What to bring

A valid ID, your HMO or PhilHealth card, and your doctor’s request if you have one.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a doctor’s request for lab tests?

No. You can request most tests yourself. If you have a request from your doctor, bring it so we follow it exactly.

How do I get my results?

Most results are emailed as a PDF the same day. You can also pick up a printed copy at the front desk.

Do you accept PhilHealth and HMOs?

Yes, for eligible services. Bring your card and a valid ID. Call ahead if you are unsure whether a test is covered.

Do you do company medical exams?

Yes. We do pre-employment and annual physical exams at the clinic or on-site for groups of 20 or more.
