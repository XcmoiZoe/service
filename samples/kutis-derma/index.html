Good to know

Questions, answered

Is diode laser painful?

Most clients feel a quick warm snap. Our machine has a cooling tip, so it is far gentler than older lasers.

How many laser sessions will I need?

Usually six to eight, spaced four to six weeks apart, for long-lasting results.

Do you have a dermatologist?

Yes. A board-certified dermatologist sees patients every Saturday by appointment.

Can I come with friends?

Of course. Add a note when booking and we will arrange back-to-back treatments for your group.
