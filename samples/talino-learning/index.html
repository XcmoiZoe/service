FAQ

Parents usually ask

Still unsure? Message us. Teacher Mae replies on weekdays.

What grade levels do you teach?

Kinder to Grade 10 for tutorials, plus Grade 6 and Grade 12 students preparing for entrance exams.

Who are the teachers?

Licensed professional teachers (LPTs) with at least two years of classroom or tutoring experience.

How many students are in a class?

A maximum of five per group, so every child gets attention. One-on-one sessions are also available.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes, by Zoom or Google Meet, at the same rates as face-to-face classes.

How will I know if my child is improving?

You get a short progress report by Messenger every month, and you can request a parent-teacher chat anytime.
