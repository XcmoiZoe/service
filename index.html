Flutter app + web dashboard · Artbi Global Philippines

YES! Free WiFi: turning free public Wi-Fi into an ad network

Challenge
Artbi Global runs free public Wi-Fi hotspots and needed a way to earn from them. They wanted to show advertisers' ads on the Wi-Fi login page, keep users coming back, and track how every campaign performs.
What I built
A Flutter app on Google Play where users log in, earn reward points and spin a daily roulette. A web dashboard for the team to manage access points, ad campaigns, locations and reports, with a live preview of every ad.

How long does a website take?

A one-page Starter site takes about 5 days. A Business site usually takes 2–3 weeks, and Shopify stores 2–4 weeks depending on products. You'll get an exact timeline with your quote.

How do payments work?

50% downpayment to start, 50% when the site is live. I accept GCash, bank transfer and PayPal. The price is fixed in your quote, so there are no surprise fees.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Business and Online Store packages are built so you can edit text, images and products yourself, and I'll show you how.

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines?

Yes. I work remotely with clients worldwide and can also be hired through Upwork if you prefer.

I already have a website. Can you fix or improve it?

Yes. I do bug fixes, redesigns, speed improvements and SEO on existing WordPress, Shopify and custom sites. Small fixes start at ₱800.

What happens after launch?

Every package includes support after launch. For ongoing help, Website care starts at ₱1,500/month and covers updates, backups and small edits.
